# Review: row 6 — score card

## Findings

- **High — safety checklist can report an unrelated later coach line as handling the flag.** `src/score/card.py:73-74` marks the safety item “yes” whenever every flag has `coach_responded=True`; the flagger defines that field as the existence of any later coach line (`src/flagger/rules.py:24,32`). A later coach line that changes the subject therefore counts as handling a safety issue. This can give managers a false assurance that a human followed up. Track an actual response to each flag or keep the item unresolved until such evidence exists.

- **Medium — the safety evidence lines are participant lines, not evidence of handling.** `src/score/card.py:74` returns flagged participant line indices as `evidence_lines` for both “yes” and “no”. When the value is “yes”, those lines establish the report that triggered the flag, not that the coach handled it. Include the responding coach line(s) as evidence, or avoid marking the item handled without such evidence.
